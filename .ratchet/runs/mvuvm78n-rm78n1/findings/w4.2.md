# w4.2 — Fused MoE Router and Cooperative Top-K Selection Kernel

## Overview
Designed and verified a fused gating and top-8 cooperative selection kernel that computes expert routing probabilities, extracts the top-8 expert indices and normalized weights, and initializes the complete MoE dispatch descriptor directly in shared memory and registers with zero intermediate global memory writes.

Artifacts and benchmarks are archived under `.ratchet/runs/mvuvm78n-rm78n1/findings/w4.2-work/`.

## Key Findings & Performance Metrics
- **Correctness & Numerics**: Validated against the PyTorch reference across all six scored challenge token regimes ($T \in \{1, 8, 16, 32, 128, 256\}$). Across 3,528 total routed slots, observed **0 ID mismatches** (100% bit-exact expert selection) and maximum weight relative L2 error $< 1.1 \times 10^{-7}$ (well within the challenge's 1.5% tolerance). Local expert histograms, exclusive prefix offsets, and fat/skinny job schedules matched identically. Evidence: `verification_results.json`.
- **Latency & Speedup vs Incumbent**:
  - At **$T = 1$ (decode 1)**: Unified single-launch execution reduced execution time from **73.43 µs** to **48.81 µs** (**1.50x speedup**, 24.62 µs reduction) by eliminating the intermediate Triton-to-HBM kernel boundary and subsequent MoE prologue re-scan.
  - At **$T = 128$ (verify 16)**: Latency dropped from **461.29 µs** to **322.71 µs** (**1.43x speedup**).
  - At **$T = 256$ (verify 32)**: Latency dropped from **906.98 µs** to **676.57 µs** (**1.34x speedup**).
  - Evidence: `incumbent_comparison.json`.
- **Memory Traffic Elimination**: Completely removes HBM traffic for `topk_ids` ($4 \times 8 \times T$ bytes), `topk_weights` ($4 \times 8 \times T$ bytes), and `local_ids` ($4 \times 8 \times T$ bytes), alongside the redundant re-parsing of routing tables across 132 SMs in the downstream MoE prologue.

## Architecture & Implementation Details
- **Cooperative Warp Top-8 Extraction**: 32 lanes partition 256 experts (8 experts/lane). Gating logits are transformed in registers via fast numerical sigmoid ($0.176776695 \cdot \text{logit}$) and biased with router bias. An 8-round cooperative tournament using monotonic unsigned float packing and warp shuffles (`__shfl_down_sync`) extracts the top-8 in 5 cycles per round with exact lexicographical tie-breaking (lower expert ID on score ties).
- **Shared Memory Dispatch Descriptor**: Direct in-Smem construction of `SmemDispatchDescriptor`:
  - `cnt[32]`: Local expert route counts.
  - `off[32]`: Exclusive prefix sum of local route offsets.
  - `ent[EMAX]` and `pos[EMAX]`: Pair-to-token and token-to-pair mappings.
  - `weights[EMAX]`: Normalized weights stored directly for expert GEMM epilogues.
  - `jE`, `jS`, `jC`, `njF`, `njS`: Pre-computed fat and skinny execution queues.
- **CUDA Graph Safety**: Zero host-device synchronizations (`.item()` / `.cpu()`) or dynamic allocations, ensuring full compatibility with CUDA graph capture.

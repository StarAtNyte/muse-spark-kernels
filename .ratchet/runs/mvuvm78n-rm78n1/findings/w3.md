# w3 summary: muse_bf16_proj (public 1.5395x, #1 1.6498x)

## What was tested
- Previous attempt (timed out at 30 min) built `experiments/projection_vjeux_router256_m1_v1`: the canonical 1.5395x source plus vjeux routes for router m256 (one fused TMA+WGMMA `rk_kernel` hi+lo launch), unpooler m1 (12-bit lossless `cgemv`) and router m1 (`rgemv`). A Modal screen showed the m=32 qkv/unpooler `sk2` routes regressing, so they were dropped.
- Official check `da14d2b1148a4849`: **1.5258x, CI [1.5029, 1.5486]**, below the public 1.5395x, so it was **not submitted**. The targeted shapes did improve (unpooler m1 2.002 vs 1.851, router m256 2.436 vs 2.205). Code paths that were not touched also moved by up to -7% (qkv m1 1.439 vs 1.541), so run-to-run noise is about ±1.5% or more.
- prepare() took 153.6 s of the 300 s limit (autotune runs in prepare). Adding more tuning candidates risks hitting the limit.

## Where the gap is (model: public per-shape speedups, `findings/w3-work/per_shape_lever.txt`)
- Bringing M=128/256 qkv/o/pooler/unpooler to 1.15x SOL alone gives **1.659x**, above #1. verify16 goes from 1.297 to 1.584 and verify32 from 1.227 to 1.575. Decode-side route tweaks are each worth less than the noise.
- Gain per shape at 1.15x SOL: qkv256 +1.4%, qkv128 +1.2%, pooler256 +1.1%, pooler128 +0.9%, router128/256 +0.8% each (rk at 4.7/6.0 us vs SOL 2.2/2.7), o +0.6-0.7%, unpooler +0.4-0.6%.
- Kernels in use: qkv256 is cuBLASLt nvjet 72x64 at 18.7 us (about 2.0 TB/s on 37.7 MB). qkv128 is the custom qb_kernel at 13.7 us. Pooler 128/256 is nvjet coopB at 21.9/25.8 us. Unpooler 128/256 is nvjet at 18.4/23.5 us.
- Likely cause for qkv: N=2304 with 128-wide M/N tiles gives only 18x2 = 36 output tiles for 132 SMs. A WGMMA kernel with split-K of 3-4 (or stream-K), a TMA multicast of x, and a single fused reduction should get it near 11-12 us.

## Next steps

3. Submit only once a check beats 1.5395x by more than the CI. A +0.3% route change is a coin flip.

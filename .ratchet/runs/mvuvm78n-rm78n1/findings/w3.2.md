# w3.2 — router rk_kernel at M=128/256: no >1 us win exists; not checked or submitted

**Result: negative.** rk (vjeux, `experiments/scored_projection_vjeux_hybrid_v1`) already does split-K (S=12/8, 96/128 CTAs)
with hi+lo wgmmas on one staged x tile. It runs at ≈5.7 us (M=128) / ≈7.0 us (M=256) of kernel time, in a harness where the
graph overhead is 5.2-5.4 us. Its pure load phase (no MMA/red) alone takes ≈4.7 / ≈6.2 us: a ~10 MB cold read runs at ≈2.1 TB/s,
and pre-packed contiguous reads are no faster (≈4.35 us for 9 MB). So the 2.2/2.7 us SOL floors are unreachable and rk is within
≈1 us of its own read time.

Tried on Modal H200 (8 paired screens, all checked with reference.check_implementation at m ∈ {M-31, M-1, M}, normal + outliers;
0 correctness failures):
1. 24 split-K tile/S configs (1-4 consumer WGs × BN 64/128/256): none faster at M=128; best at M=256 is a tie (-0.13 us). Larger
   tiles lose 1-9 us because red.add volume and contention grow.
2. W pre-packed for 1-D bulk loads: ≤0.2 us, noise level.
3. int8-lo (lo = q·2^(E_hi−142), W 8→6 MB): correct (weight rms 6.6e-6) but the smem expansion costs 2-5 us. Even with the
   expansion skipped entirely, it gains only +0.4-0.6 us, below the 1 us bar.

So no official check was run and nothing was submitted. Evidence, raw logs and code: `findings/w3.2-work/` (NOTES.md, screen1-8 logs).

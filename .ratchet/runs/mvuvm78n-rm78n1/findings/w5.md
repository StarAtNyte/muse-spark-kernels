## Core Findings

1. **Fused Prefill K64 MoE Path (`experiments/prefill_k64_fused_v1/`)**:
   - Primary evidence: `experiments/prefill_k64_fused_v1/bench.log` and `hypothesis.md`.
   - Result: Correctness passed (rel L2 ~1.4–1.7e-4), but cold-L2 full-layer timings yielded a geometric mean of **1.0019x** across 4 representative shapes with regressions in 2 cases (`batch 4, q 753, full`: 0.9992x; `batch 1, q 16384, full ctx 0`: 0.9950x).
   - Root cause: The shared-memory cross-thread exchange and block barrier required during activation gather negate the scale selection decoding gains.
   - Decision: **Archive only; do not spend official check quota.**

2. **Attention Component Benchmark (`.ratchet/runs/mvuvm78n-rm78n1/findings/w5-work/test_attn_bench.py`)**:
   - Screened all 8 scored prefill cases on Modal H200 (`ap-r23oyq3FYAY4C9Br6zc5rz`).
   - The custom Hopper kernel (`attn_fwd_kernel`) is **1.05x to 1.18x faster** than FlashAttention-3 (`sgl_kernel.flash_attn.flash_attn_with_kvcache`) across all 8 shapes (e.g. 31.1 µs vs 36.7 µs on `turn1_slide`, 367.6 µs vs 429.4 µs on `turn1_full`, 309.6 µs vs 334.4 µs on `ft_slide_32k`, and 6772.8 µs vs 7974.1 µs on `ft_full_64k`).
   - Advantage comes from fusing QK-norm, RoPE, FP8 attention, and the post-norm gate epilogue in registers/SRAM, avoiding the intermediate activation DRAM roundtrip required by FA3.

3. **Dense GEMMs Benchmark (`.ratchet/runs/mvuvm78n-rm78n1/findings/w5-work/test_qkv_lt.py`)**:
   - Screened T=16384 QKV projection on Modal H200 (`ap-19J0OIi3WvMGrvsHBQjD2e`).
   - `torch.mm` runs at 764.88 µs (808.6 TFLOP/s, ~82% peak SM90 BF16 rate).
   - Best cuBLASLt heuristic algorithm (`Algo 1`, tile id 24) runs at 757.90 µs (1.009x speedup, delta 7.0 µs), representing a negligible <0.08% whole-layer speedup.
   - Challenge rules require lossless BF16 weights and BF16 activations at boundaries; FP8 weight quantization is disallowed.

# Worker 4.1 Findings: Fused RMSNorm into Projection Prologue with Exact BF16 Intermediate Bounds

## Executive Summary
This investigation resolves the critical open bottleneck identified in `decoder.md`: eliminating the intermediate HBM activation round trips and separate grid launch between the post-attention residual boundary and the dense projections (`pooler` and `router`), while maintaining exact numerical adherence to whole-decoder reference checks across all 12 evaluation shapes.

1. **Failure Mode of Prior Algebraic Shortcut (GLE_T)**:
   The previous attempt (`GLE_T`, `submission.py` line 13230) pulled the scalar normalization factor $r_2$ out of the linear projection:
   $$W \cdot (r_2 \cdot (\alpha s + \beta r_1 P)) = r_2 \alpha (W \cdot s) + r_2 \beta r_1 (W \cdot P)$$
   Primary evidence proves this approach is fundamentally flawed for $T \ge 8$: it splits the projection into **two independent GEMMs** ($W \cdot u$ and $W \cdot v$), doubling total FLOPs and doubling memory traffic for the 67.1 MB Pooler weights (134.2 MB vs 67.1 MB). That $2\times$ memory penalty completely overwhelms any launch overhead savings for $T \ge 8$.

2. **The Fused Prologue Solution**:
   Instead of deferring normalization past the GEMM, the normalization calculation is placed directly into the **prologue of the single GEMM/GEMV kernel**:
   - Computes RMSNorm on $s_1$ in FP32 on-chip using warp shuffles and shared memory reduction.
   - Preserves **exact BF16 intermediate rounding** via `__float2bfloat16_rn` in registers/SRAM before issuing WGMMA instructions.
   - For $T \le 8$, stages unquantized FP32 $h_{\text{exact}}$ for the router, ensuring 100% router top-8 expert fidelity.
   - Executes **exactly one GEMM** ($W \cdot h_{\text{bf16}}$), eliminating intermediate DRAM writes and reads for activations.

3. **Empirical Numerical Validation (All 12 Evaluation Shapes)**:
   Verified experimentally via `prologue_fusion_direct.py` against `reference.py` and `checks.py`:
   - All 12 evaluation shapes pass `check_routing` (100% pass rate).
   - Relative L2 error on Pooler is $1.60 \times 10^{-3}$ to $1.67 \times 10^{-3}$, well inside the task tolerance ($0.091$ to $0.144$).
   - Top-8 expert match is 100% for $T \in \{1, 8, 16\}$ and $96.9\text{--}98.5\%$ for $T \in \{32, 128, 256\}$ (mismatches occur exclusively on near-tie scores within $0.015$, far below the $0.05$ threshold allowed by `ROUTE_TOL`).

4. **Performance Impact & Speedup Projection**:
   - Saves **3.50 µs** of kernel launch and synchronization latency per layer.
   - Saves **0.02 µs to 2.62 µs** of HBM memory streaming time (eliminating 64 KB to 12.3 MB of intermediate activation traffic per step).
   - Net latency reduction per shape: **3.52 µs** (Decode 1) to **6.12 µs** (Verify 32).
   - Incremental geometric mean speedup across all 6 loads: **1.02841x** (+2.84%).
   - Projected decoder score increases from incumbent **2.26032x** to **2.32453x**, crossing the submission threshold.

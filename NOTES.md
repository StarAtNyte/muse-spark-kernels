- SMOE v2/v3 ablations (experiments/moe_smoe_dyn_v2, moe_smoe_abl_v3, Modal H200, paired vs parent): static round-robin claims = same speed as dynamic (claims not the cause). bs1(3 experts)/bs8(10): full 46.1/133.5 us; no-compute 36.3/95.5; no-epilogue 33.8/96.3; 4-way split bulk copies 46.5/134.5; parent 29.1/76.5.
  -> per-unit epilogue (global stores + __threadfence under saturated DRAM) costs ~25%; even without it this 1-CTA/SM 36 KB-unit TMA ring with K-split over 8 warps is 15-25% slower than the parent TINY/TINY2 build. REJECTED after 3 screens (wild budget). If revisited: dedicated reducer warps, one release per expert change instead of a fence per unit, and profile why the bare ring streams only ~2.5 TB/s.
- Attention (experiments/attention_xu_offload_v1, Modal paired vs readable baseline 12167249d81c46ee): full-verify is latency-bound in the per-tile QK->softmax->PV chain, not in MUFU exp or FP8 cvt. Rejected: polynomial exp offload 25/50/100% (0.955/0.86/0.72x), FP16 probability path (0.96x), early half-PV issue (1.00x). Next lever: FA3-style QK(i+1) before softmax(i), needs ~32 more regs -> 1-CTA/SM rewrite.
- Decoder T=1/T=8 (experiments/decoder_smallT_unfin_v1, Modal paired vs decoder_readable_fp32_ybuf_v4): projections already stream near their practical floors; the slack is at kernel boundaries (post-MoE unpooler+_sl_cluster_finish 5.2 us vs ~1.5 us floor; T=8 glue_cluster_boundary ~3.2 us). Rejected: L2 prefetch during attention (0.98-1.009x), PCX_CFG pre-wait depths (0.984-1.007x), merging unpooler+finish via an in-kernel global counter barrier (0.944-0.963x: the barrier costs more than the launch it removes). The T=1 fused o+boundary path can't run at T=8 (FP32 router GEMV limited to 8 rows).
- MoE small-batch timeline re-measured (experiments/moe_tail_stripe_v1): bs1 2 experts: route 2.3, first unit 3.07, A done 10.8-13.1, B done 17.7-19.2, barrier 20.0, final 20.7 us. The earlier 5.7 us phase-A spread is now 1.5-2 us, just 7 vs 8 units per CTA (8 is already the minimum max). Routing is mostly overlapped by pre-issued units. Overhead sits in the A->B hand-off (~2.8 us) and the tail (~1.5 us). Rejected: two-segment schedule (0.96-0.98x), 16 compute warps (0.88-0.94x, register cap 96). Fence-free hand-off -DOKC_DF=1: ~0 at bs1, ~+1.5% at bs8 (below bar). Code edits alone shift timing 3-4%; always run a same-code control.
- Conclusion 2026-11-03: the small-batch MoE, the decoder projections and attention are each near their current design's limits; remaining gains need structural rewrites (attention FA3-style QK/softmax overlap at 1 CTA/SM; T=8 fused pooler/router to 16 rows; MoE end-of-kernel tail).

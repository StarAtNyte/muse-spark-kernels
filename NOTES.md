- MoE small-batch timeline re-measured (experiments/moe_tail_stripe_v1): bs1 2 experts: route 2.3, first unit 3.07, A done 10.8-13.1, B done 17.7-19.2, barrier 20.0, final 20.7 us. - Conclusion 2026-11-03: the small-batch MoE, the decoder projections and attention are each near their current design's limits; remaining gains need structural rewrites (attention FA3-style QK/softmax overlap at 1 CTA/SM; T=8 fused pooler/router to 16 rows; MoE end-of-kernel tail).
- Decoder T=8 fused gle path (experiments/decoder_gle8_v1): 16-row pooler+FP32 router build correct 13/13 but verify-1 0.89-0.93x vs same-code control. It saves the ~4.3 us boundary launch, but the pooler goes 20.0 -> 28.0 us (deferred norm doubles rows: 30% more smem bytes per CTA) and the route kernel ends 5-8 us after the pooler. Fused gle only pays at T=1.
- Decoder KV prefetch before griddepcontrol.wait (experiments/decoder_kvpre_v1): the full-layer flat kernel's producers already gather KV without waiting. Adding the same early start to the per-request kernels (B=1 and sliding) gave 0.997x geomean (0.994-0.999 per load): the KV latency was already hidden behind q load/RoPE, and QKV + KV both run at full bandwidth (max ~0.6% from overlap).
- Decoder _graft_row_boundary_pre (experiments/decoder_rowpre_v1): its 4.6 us/call in profiles is mostly the PDL wait for the sk2 projection to drain. Its real cost is ~2.8 us/call at T=32 (a fixed latency chain of two dependent row reductions), ~3.5/5.3 us at T=128/256 (near the BW floor). More CTAs (8-CTA cluster kernel) 0.9998x; fewer warps flat; stream preload 1.0016x. Only fusing it into a neighbor would help.
- Attention FA3-style rewrite (experiments/attention_fa3pp_v1): look-ahead QK before softmax at 1 CTA/SM (232 regs, 8 stages) 0.75x; old loop at 1 CTA/SM 0.81-0.83x; deferred-PV wait 0.79-0.81x (1 CTA/SM) / 0.97x (2 CTA/SM). Full verify is NOT limited by wgmma latency. It needs warp-level parallelism (4 warpgroups/SM); the remaining cost is per-score issue work (exp, cvt, barriers). Next lever, if any: fewer instructions per score, or more resident warps.

# Judge environment parity

The [challenge rules](https://openkernelchallenge.dev/rules) identify `lmsysorg/sglang:dev-muse-spark-open`, one H200 SXM for kernels, and the installed PyTorch, Triton, FlashInfer, DeepGEMM, TileLang, CuTe DSL and CUTLASS stack. The official judge alternates candidate and SGLang CUDA-graph replays on the same inputs. Official status records include an internal image digest (for example `experiments/scored_moe_wgs_gathb7_v1/status.json`); the public tag is mutable and its identity with that internal digest is **not established**.

Our earlier component runners use `nvidia/cuda:13.0.2-devel-ubuntu22.04`, Python 3.12 and `torch==2.11.0` plus independently installed Triton and NumPy. They use an H200, but are not the judge image, and some stub SGLang or omit FlashInfer/FA3. Drivers have different inputs, event overhead and cache flush behavior. Therefore treat those as screens, not exact official reproduction; never directly compare their absolute times to overhead-corrected official times.

`dev/modal/judge_env.py` inspects the publicly specified image on H200 without deliberately installing packages. `dev/modal/run_judge_image.py` provides a bounded component-driver runner on the same public tag. Modal necessarily wraps the image with its execution environment; the public tag and Modal driver are not proof of equivalence with the service's deployed image, driver, clock policy, randomization, benchmark harness or hidden inputs. Run official checks to verify full compatibility and score. The image audit's raw output is `research/judge-image-audit.log`; inspect the outcome before claiming success or using the runner.

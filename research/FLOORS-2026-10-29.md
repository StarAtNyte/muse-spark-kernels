# Measured H200 floors and today's results (2026-10-29)

All measurements on Modal H200 (cold L2, CUDA graphs, paired where comparing). Each item links its evidence.

## Hardware floors
- Contiguous read: TMA bulk 4x32 KB / 8x16 KB per CTA, 1 CTA/SM: 3.8-3.9 TB/s incl. launch, ~4.05-4.13 TB/s
  minus launch at 340-425 MB; ~2.7 TB/s at 56 MB (launch/ramp dominated). Model: bytes / 4.1 TB/s + 5-7 us.
  (`experiments/h200_read_methods_v1`)
- Random 128 B row gather (KV page=1 layout): 3.6-3.76 TB/s at 655 MB; small gathers: 80K rows 13.2 us, 2,048 rows
  7.0 us event-timed (empty graph 5.5 us). (`experiments/h200_gather_bw_v1`)
- Extra concurrent traffic (L2 prefetch, deeper rings, more PDL pre-wait stages) consistently slows DRAM-bound
  chains: `moe_pfb_screen_v1`, `moe_sk1_screen_v1` (NS), `decoder_pdln_screen_v1`, `attention_stages_v1`.

## Where each board stands vs floors
- MoE: kernel within 3-9% of a naive strided stream, 13-33% above the contiguous-TMA floor
  (`moe_stream_floor_diag_v1`, `moe_timeline_v1`). WGS bs32: ~2.4 lane-instr per FP4 weight, 57% issue-active,
  DRAM 3.27 TB/s (`moe_wgs_stalls_v1`). Scale bytes are 11.8% of expert bytes; dropping them all would gain only
  3-7% (`moe_scale_ablation_v1`).
- Decoder: MoE is ~35-55% of the layer at T=1/16/32 (`decoder_ablation_v1`); dense/attention routes at T<=32
  already match the projection stack (`decoder_dense_small_v1`).
- Attention: decode-32 full at the random-gather limit; single-request cases 2-6 us above gather floors
  (`attention_small_floor_v1`), but plans/stages/zhebrak hybrid do not help (`attention_plan_b1_v1`,
  `attention_stages_v1`, `attention_zhebrak_pair_v1`). ncu: 1.65 TB/s at decode-1 full (`attention_small_ncu_v1`).
- Projections: wide2 has no material headroom over current M128/M256 routes (`projection_wide2_sweep_v1`).

## Results
- Improvement: decoder T=128 qkv + T=128/256 o via the projection stack: check 2.0999x, scored 2.080x (noise);
  canonical (`decoder_bigproj_*`, `scored_decoder_bigproj_v1`).
- Rejected (all measured): phase-B L2 prefetch, single-token split-K MoE (correct, 16% slower), FT_T=32 decoder MoE
  (ptxas register failure), MoE build swap at T=16/32, WGS knobs in decoder, sk2 PDL pre-wait depth, attention plan
  overrides and pipeline depth, zhebrak attention hybrid (published judge numbers not comparable across revisions).

## Implication
The remaining large lever is a structurally different MoE kernel that streams expert weights near 4 TB/s for 16-256
tokens (the leaders are ~15% ahead on MoE and decoder, i.e. near the TMA floor). Fewer instructions per weight are
needed: the current decode + scale path costs ~2 instructions per weight.

## External: OpenBMB/JustGoal (checked 2026-10-29)
Goal-only agent kernels for B200 (sm_100a, tcgen05/TMEM, CuTe DSL 4.6). Overlapping operators: gqa_paged_decode
(page 1, bf16) and moe_fp8_fwd (FP8 weights). Their page-1 decode design (split-KV ~8x SM count, GQA heads packed
into MMA M, fused last-CTA merge with self-resetting counters for CUDA graphs) matches what our attention already
does on sm_90; the MoE is FP8/Blackwell. Nothing new to transfer to the H200 judge.

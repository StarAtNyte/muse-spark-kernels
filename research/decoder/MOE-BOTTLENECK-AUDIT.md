# Decoder MoE bottleneck audit — 2026-11-02

The earlier statement that decoder MoE is approximately 1.6× above its streaming floor uses a **theoretical 4.8 TB/s byte-count bound**, not an observed streaming floor. It should not be read as proof that 37.5% of MoE time is recoverable. The decoder diagnostic measured 68/98 µs for decode batches 16/32 against 41/59 µs at 4.8 TB/s; these are different routing samples, so their ratio is indicative rather than an achievable speedup.

A separate standalone MoE experiment measured actual cold-L2 reads on its own expert selections: for batch 16 the full kernel took 102–110 µs versus 99–107 µs for a simple stream; for batch 32 it took 130.5 µs versus 119.9 µs. Those figures do not directly bound the decoder, whose selected experts and weight traffic differ. They do show that the same workload can approach a measured read-only stream, making a large improvement from reducing arithmetic alone uncertain. Evidence: `experiments/moe_stream_floor_diag_v1/FINDINGS.md` and `experiments/decoder_moe_floor_diag_v1/modal.log`.

The NCU sample at standalone batch 32 measured 3.27 TB/s DRAM, 57% scheduler issue activity, and substantial scoreboard, GMMA, and math-pipe stalls. Instrumented consumer waits accounted for roughly 10–15% of phase time. These observations support investigating both instruction issue and the memory pipeline; neither independently proves the limiting cause. Evidence: `experiments/moe_wgs_stalls_v1/FINDINGS.md` and `experiments/moe_wgs_waits_v1/FINDINGS.md`.

For a useful next measurement, collect the read-only stream and the integrated decoder MoE on **the same weights, exact routed expert IDs, cache-flush method, and H200 run**, then profile the actual decoder kernel at M=16 and M=32. Compare effective bytes, DRAM bandwidth, eligible warps and wait breakdown. This separates an attainable memory target from the theoretical bound and avoids transferring conclusions from different router seeds or standalone inputs. Any candidate must then pass whole-layer correctness and paired full-layer timing before an official check.

Matched same-run decoder measurements are now in `experiments/decoder_moe_matched_stream_v1/`: the MoE exceeded a read-only stream of the same active packed experts by 7–17 µs across four M=16/32 samples. The stream is a comparison, not a guaranteed lower bound.

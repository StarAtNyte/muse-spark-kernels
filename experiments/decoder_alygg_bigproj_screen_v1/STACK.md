# Integrated decoder projection stacking

The alygg77 integrated decoder is the promoted parent. Our previously measured projection stack had a T128 QKV and T128/T256 output projection improvement, but appending the entire projection stack made a 1,241,100-byte submission, rejected above the judge's 1,000,000-byte limit (`experiments/decoder_alygg_bigproj_v1/`).

Extracted only the actual MIT-licensed Nathen QKV and O backend code from our prior projection stack, retaining the full MIT notice in each candidate. QKV-only candidate `experiments/decoder_alygg_compact_qkv128_v1/submission.py` is 852,300 bytes; official check `9a3e2477765a4b30`. The QKV+O candidate `experiments/decoder_alygg_compact_qkv_o_v1/submission.py` is 859,525 bytes; official check `eb606b3d40a240ca`. Both target T128 only; previous T256 O route was cuBLASLt rather than this extracted O kernel, so it is not included. The second candidate adds the exact 128-thread-tile O configuration `(128,128,4,3,1,1)` used by the earlier route. Full checks are needed for preparation limits, numerical behavior and integrated dispatch; no speedup is claimed yet. Preserve both job IDs and do not relaunch on observation timeout.

Dispatch audit: the integrated decoder's outer `kernel_fn` can use a `graft_lt` QKV plan before reaching the compact QKV hook, so the first two checks may bypass that part of the change. Forced candidate `experiments/decoder_alygg_forced_qkv_o_v1/submission.py` removes the T128 graft QKV plan in prepared state, selects the branch that calls the compact hook, and fails preparation if the compact route is unavailable; it also contains the T128 O route. Official check `d8056151187a4b7b` is pending. The O route may also be bypassed by higher-level fused dispatch; inspect profiling before attributing a timing change. These are distinct candidates, not repetitions of one job.

QKV-only check `9a3e2477765a4b30` passed at 2.21377× (95% CI 2.18056–2.24697). Its verify-16 profile shows cuBLAS nvjet kernels and no identifiable qbig kernel, consistent with the graft dispatch bypass; the profile's truncated names mean this alone is not definitive. Its aggregate advantage is within run-to-run uncertainty, so it does not establish a stacked speedup. The forced-dispatch candidate remains the meaningful test.

No timed cases ran. The forced version shares that bug. This guard does not prove the changed route executes in scored cases; inspect its profile.

# M32 QKV specialization from the M16 compressed kernel

Starting from the highest-scored projection source SHA-256 `d12bdc54efaa1c8eca185c723a97188ba7e303d67a1671597a3a722500ba7de9`, the H200 driver regenerated the M16 compressed-QKV instance with `MT=4` instead of 2 to admit 32 rows. It reused the prepared weight representation and compared its direct launch with the incumbent full `kernel_fn`. H200 public-image app `ap-WTPcw4ciaqejVPc7XQj6dD` passed the reference for both arms on seeds 6121 and 6122. Paired cold-L2 median baseline/candidate times across 24 replays were 16.544/21.152 and 16.672/21.232 µs, respectively. Thus this specialization is about 27–28% slower in this two-seed screen and is rejected without an official submission. The direct candidate and baseline wrappers are not identical; the large regression is still clear in this screen. SGLang was stubbed; no official score is claimed. Exact runtime edit and driver: `driver.py`; raw output: `screen.log`.

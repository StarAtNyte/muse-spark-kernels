# Projection score sensitivity

`analyze.py` reads all 30 per-shape medians from published job `beb3e26afb2544c9` (1.539506×). The task uses six equally weighted loads; all five projection cases in each load have equal weight. For a single shape with median t and load sum T, a hypothetical latency reduction fraction d changes the overall score by `(T / (T - d*t))**(1/6) - 1`, holding all other cases and baseline times fixed. `results.txt` ranks the projected impact of a 10% improvement to each individual shape.

The two largest sensitivities are decode-1 and verify-1 pooler (0.658% and 0.628%), though their measured latencies are already below the reference's nominal traffic estimate and further improvement needs a justified algorithmic or cache model. Among larger verify cases, pooler M128 and M256 yield 0.527% and 0.519% projected score gain for a hypothetical 10% per-case improvement. Their medians exceed the reference SOL estimates by 5.68 and 7.83 µs, respectively. The reference SOL values are idealized estimates, not mathematical lower bounds or promises of attainable performance. Score effects are estimates using rounded, separate per-shape medians and omit variance, changes in other shapes and interactions; they are not measured gains. Targeting pooler requires comparing against the actual incumbent route and official correctness/timing.
